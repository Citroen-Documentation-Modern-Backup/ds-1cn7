Repair: Force limiter

XSARA II · Repair procedures · SINCE RPO 8688 · Precautions to be taken · Reference C5JK0AP0

The front seat belts must be replaced if the vehicle has been subjected to an impact that has triggered the airbag - the torsion bar of the load limiter will be deformed. The deformation may not however be visible.
